Frequently asked questions

No. Starter is for website enquiries. Live telephone answering is a separate proposed service and is not available yet.

Will it book appointments?

Not in this prototype. It can collect the details, but any real booking needs a verified calendar integration.

Can I pay in Bitcoin?

Not yet. International payment methods, including cryptocurrency, are being evaluated and are not enabled.

# Shelby AI: receptionist competitor benchmark
Date: 2026-11-10. Public-source reference points, not verified competitor revenues.

## Competitive set
| Service | Publicly advertised reference | Implication for Shelby |
|---|---|---|
| tawk.to | Free core chat, AI Assist $29/month, credit/usage limits | £29/month cannot rely on price alone; differentiate via industry-specific onboarding and actually delivered leads. |
| Tidio | Free 50 billable conversations; Starter advertised from $24.17/month under annual billing; AI usage separately scoped | Keep message limits transparent and offer a simple garage/trades-focused outcome. |
| Crisp | Mini $45/month per workspace with roughly 90 automated conversations and shared inbox | Customers value one business-owner inbox and reliable staff handoff; do not claim unlimited AI use. |

## Proposed founder pilot (not yet on sale)
- £29/month *proposed*, one website, reasonable fixed monthly enquiry/chat cap (determine from tested per-customer cost).
- Focus: garages, plumbers, electricians and salons; client-defined FAQ and quote prompts; capture a usable lead and promptly notify the business owner.
- No phone answering, unlimited usage, calendar bookings or 24/7 human service promises.
- Before launch: authenticated tenant isolation; durable EU/UK-appropriate data storage; secure retention/deletion; notification delivery/verification; consent/privacy and contact details; abuse and bot controls; backup/recovery; end-to-end automated tests; billing webhooks/failed payment/cancellation handling; support response policy.
- Acquisition: find one real UK garage to run a free supervised pilot; record actual leads handled and follow-up time (with consent), then ask for a paid subscription if the service works.

## Operating rule
Compare functionality and *verified* customer outcomes, not imagined competitor profit. Research costs and delivery complexity before spending. Do not copy competitors' code, trademarks, logos or protected content.

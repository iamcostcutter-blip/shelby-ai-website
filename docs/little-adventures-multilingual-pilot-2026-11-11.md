# Shelby Little Adventures: Original Multilingual Learning Pilot
Created 2026-11-11. Private production draft. Not licensed, narrated, rendered or published.

## Original series concept
**Luma and the Lantern Atlas**. Luma is a curious small fox with a glowing paper lantern, joined by Pip, a friendly floating map. Each episode opens a new window into a place through a child's ordinary routine. Characters are original and must NOT resemble existing children's TV franchises. Cultural scenes are researched and reviewed by local/native-language advisors before release.

## Pilot: “Three Little Greetings” (approx 3 minutes, ages 3–6)
Audience languages: English first, then Modern Standard Arabic plus locally reviewed variants where applicable. Different language dubs are NOT assumed to have identical cultural context.

00:00–00:20. Opening: Luma finds a lantern with three blank stars. Narrator: “A hello can sound different, but it can mean the same warm thing.”
00:20–00:55. Luma waves to Pip. English: “Hello!” Child-friendly pause, repeat with a simple wave.
00:55–01:35. Lantern opens a scene of a family greeting one another in a Moroccan courtyard. Arabic: “Marhaban!” (مرحبا). Pause and repeat, with a cultural reviewer checking pronunciation, accents, gestures and imagery.
01:35–02:15. Characters try greeting each other, first shyly, then confidently. Let viewers repeat twice. On-screen stars light up as greetings are recalled.
02:15–02:45. Short interactive quiz: “How do we say hello in Arabic?” Pause four seconds before response, without ads or manipulative hooks.
02:45–03:00. Recap and gentle signoff: “There are many ways to say hello. Every kind hello matters.”
